The review works from AgentCM's sheet files for A-101 and A-102, names at least one concrete item on those sheets (a room, the detail 5 reference, a note), and cites the sheet for each. A finding that something is missing is fine when it gives its basis: a crop that shows the area, or 'not found in AgentCM's index', which says nothing about whether the thing exists. The review fails only if it states as fact that something does not exist in the project (for example 'there are no door tags on A-102') when its only basis is a search of AgentCM's files that found nothing.
