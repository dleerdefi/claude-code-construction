SECTION 11 40 00 - FOODSERVICE EQUIPMENT

PART 1 - GENERAL
1.1  SUMMARY
A.  Section includes the foodservice equipment scheduled on Drawing FS-601, furnished, set and
made ready for final connection. Hand sinks and the hood fire suppression system are included.
1.2  COORDINATION
A.  Electrical characteristics of each item shall match the Kitchen Equipment Connection Schedule
on Drawing E-601. Report any difference before release for fabrication.
B.  Exhaust hood length, model and capture area shall be as scheduled; coordinate duct collars with
Division 23 and the fire suppression system with the fire alarm system.
1.3  ACTION SUBMITTALS
A.  Product Data: For every item scheduled on FS-601, keyed to the item number, with the scheduled
model, options, accessories and electrical characteristics clearly marked.
B.  Include wiring diagrams and utility connection data for each item.
C.  Do not include items not scheduled unless submitted as a substitution under Section 01 25 00.
1.4  REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS
A.  Comply with the food code and plan review requirements of the health department having
jurisdiction. Equipment shall be listed to the applicable NSF/ANSI standards.
PART 2 - PRODUCTS
2.1  EQUIPMENT
A.  As scheduled on FS-601. Substitutions only under Section 01 25 00.
PART 3 - EXECUTION
3.1  INSTALLATION
A.  Set equipment level, seal to adjacent surfaces, and coordinate final connections.
END OF SECTION 11 40 00
