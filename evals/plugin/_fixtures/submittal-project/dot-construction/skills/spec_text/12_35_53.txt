SECTION 12 35 53 - LABORATORY CASEWORK

PART 1 - GENERAL
1.1  SUMMARY
A.  Section includes metal laboratory casework, epoxy resin work surfaces and wall-hung counters.
B.  Fume hoods are specified in Section 11 53 13. Sinks are furnished under Section 22 40 00.
1.2  COORDINATION
A.  Furnish concealed in-wall counter brackets to the framing contractor for installation
before gypsum board. Coordinate locations and heights shown on the Drawings.
B.  Size sink cutouts to the sink models scheduled on the plumbing drawings.
1.3  ACTION SUBMITTALS
A.  Product Data: For casework, work surfaces and hardware.
B.  Shop Drawings: Plans and elevations keyed to the Contract Drawings, sections, work
surface heights, in-wall supports, cutouts for sinks and fixtures furnished by others,
and service fixture locations. Include every elevation shown on the Drawings.
C.  Samples: Work surface colors.
PART 2 - PRODUCTS
2.1  METAL CASEWORK
A.  Steel laboratory casework conforming to SEFA 8-M.
2.2  EPOXY RESIN WORK SURFACES
A.  Epoxy resin, 1 inch thick, conforming to SEFA 3.
2.3  ACCESSIBLE WORKSTATIONS
A.  Provide accessible student stations where indicated on the Drawings, with open knee space.
PART 3 - EXECUTION
3.1  INSTALLATION
A.  Install casework level and plumb. Anchor wall-hung counters to in-wall brackets.
END OF SECTION 12 35 53
