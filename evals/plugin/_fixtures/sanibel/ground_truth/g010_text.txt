PROJECT SUMMARY
THIS PROJECT CONSISTS OF A NEW TWO-STORY FIRE STATION FACILITY.  THIS BUILDING WILL BE CONSTRUCTED
AFTER THE DEMOLITION OF AN EXISTING ONE-STORY FIRE STATION ON THE SITE, AND WILL ADHERE TO THE
CURRENT COASTAL DESIGN STANDARDS.  THIS WILL BE A RISK CATEGORY IV, LEVEL 'E' ESSENTIAL FACILITY. IN
ADDITION TO THE MAIN FIRE STATION AN EXISTING GARAGE USED FOR STORAGE TO BE REFINISHED AND REMAIN.
