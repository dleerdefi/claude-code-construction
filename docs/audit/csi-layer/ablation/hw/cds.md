# Contract Document Excerpts — Severn Run Elementary School, Classroom Wing Addition

**Owner:** Chesapeake County Public Schools (CCPS), Maryland (fictional district)
**Project No.:** CCPS-2026-014 | **Location:** Severn Run, Maryland
**Architect:** Harbor Line Architects, LLC | **Construction Documents:** 13 Feb 2026; Addendum No. 2 (10 Mar 2026) reissued A-601
**Facility:** K-12 elementary school (education.k12). Two-story classroom wing addition joined to the existing 1998 building at Corridor 100. The addition is separated from the existing building by a 2-hour fire barrier at grid line 7 (see A-101, A-401).
**Jurisdiction note:** Project is in the State of Maryland. The State and the Owner's school-security office publish guidance on school safety and classroom door locking; the Architect has coordinated scheduled lock functions with the Owner's security office and the AHJ. *Any proposed change to a scheduled lock function shall be referred to the Architect and the Owner's security office before approval.* (The requirements of that guidance are not restated in these documents.)

## SECTION 08 71 00 — DOOR HARDWARE (excerpts)

### PART 1 — GENERAL

**1.1 SUMMARY**
A. Section includes finish hardware for swinging doors scheduled on A-601, including electrified hardware components and low-voltage power supplies listed in the hardware sets.
B. Related sections:
   1. 08 11 13 Hollow Metal Doors and Frames (frame/door preparation for hardware, including electrified prep).
   2. 08 14 16 Flush Wood Doors.
   3. 08 34 73 Sound Control Door Assemblies (acoustic doors D-112, D-113; gasketing and automatic door bottoms by that section).
   4. 08 41 13 Aluminum-Framed Entrances and Storefronts — hardware for storefront doors in Vestibule 100A is furnished by that section and is NOT part of this section.
   5. 26 05 00 Common Work Results for Electrical (120 V circuits, conduit, raceway, and low-voltage cable installation).
   6. 28 10 00 Access Control (card readers, door position switches, access control panel, request-to-exit interface).
   7. 28 46 00 Fire Detection and Alarm (release of hold-open devices, monitoring).
   8. 01 25 00 Substitution Procedures.

**1.3 SUBMITTALS**
A. Product Data: manufacturer's catalog cuts for each item, marked to show the exact product, function, size, options, and finish proposed.
B. Door Hardware Schedule:
   1. Vertical format, organized by hardware set number matching the sets in Part 3.4 of this Section. Under each set heading list every door assigned to the set by door mark, room name/number, door size, door type, frame type, fire rating, and handing, all as shown on A-601.
   2. Under each set list every item of hardware with: quantity, item description, manufacturer, catalog/model number, ANSI/BHMA function designation (for locks, latches and exit devices), size, finish, fasteners, and a "Furnished by / Installed by" column for any item furnished or installed under another section.
   3. Include a door-by-door index cross-referencing every door mark on A-601 to its set in the schedule. Flag any door on A-601 not covered and any discrepancy between A-601 and the sets.
   4. Hardware schedules submitted in horizontal format or without the door index will be returned without review.
C. Keying Schedule: submit WITH the hardware schedule, as a single submittal package. The keying meeting (1.4.C) shall be held before the hardware schedule is submitted so that the keying schedule reflects the Owner's decisions. A hardware schedule submitted without the keying schedule will be returned "Revise and Resubmit."
D. Electrified Hardware:
   1. Point-to-point wiring diagram for each electrified opening, showing every device at the opening (exit device, strike, EPT, door position switch, card reader, request-to-exit, hold-open devices), the power supply serving it, the fire-alarm interface (relay or module) where required, and the interface terminals at the access control panel. Show voltage, wire count and gauge for each run, and name the Section responsible for furnishing and installing each wire run and each device.
   2. Elevation of each electrified door and frame showing location of EPT, strike, and door position switch preparations, for use by 08 11 13.
   3. Power supply schedule listing each power supply, its location (per Division 26 drawings), input voltage, output voltage, rated amperage, connected load per door, and fire-alarm input where required.
   4. Diagrams that show a device "by others" without naming the Section, or that stop at a junction box without showing the panel interface, are incomplete and will be returned.
E. Templates: within 10 days after the hardware schedule is approved, furnish templates and preparation drawings to 08 11 13, 08 14 16 and 08 34 73 so door and frame fabrication can proceed.
F. Warranty: ten years for closers, three years for exit devices and electrified devices, one year other.

**1.4 QUALITY ASSURANCE**
A. Supplier: a firm that has supplied hardware for not fewer than five K-12 projects in the last five years, employing an Architectural Hardware Consultant (AHC) who prepares and signs the hardware schedule.
B. Pre-installation conference before installation of frames with electrified prep, attended by hardware supplier, 08 11 13 installer, 26 05 00, 28 10 00, 28 46 00 and the GC.
C. Keying Meeting: hardware supplier shall convene a keying meeting with the Owner's facilities and security representatives and the Architect before the hardware schedule is submitted. Record decisions and incorporate them in the keying schedule.

**1.5 COORDINATION**
A. Furnish hardware templates and electrified prep information to door and frame manufacturers.
B. Conduit, raceway, 120 V branch circuits and low-voltage cable are by 26 05 00; access control devices by 28 10 00; fire-alarm relays by 28 46 00. This Section furnishes EPTs, power supplies, electrified exit devices, electromagnetic holders and their interface relays, and provides the wiring diagrams those sections work from.

**2.2 HINGES**
A. Full mortise, five-knuckle, ball bearing, ANSI/BHMA A156.1 Grade 1, with non-removable pins (NRP) at every outswinging exterior door and at every door that swings out of a lockable room toward the corridor.
B. Weight and size:
   1. Standard weight, 4-1/2 in x 4-1/2 in, 0.134 in leaf: doors up to 3 ft 6 in wide, up to 7 ft 6 in high, and up to 150 lb.
   2. Heavy weight, 5 in x 4-1/2 in, 0.180 in leaf: doors wider than 3 ft 6 in, or higher than 7 ft 6 in, or heavier than 150 lb, all exterior doors, and all doors in 90-minute openings.
C. Quantity: two hinges per leaf up to 60 in high plus one hinge for each additional 30 in of height or fraction thereof (7 ft 0 in doors: three; 8 ft 0 in doors: four).
D. Finish: 652 interior; 630 (stainless) exterior and at Kitchen 111.

**2.3 BORED LOCKS AND LATCHES**
A. ANSI/BHMA A156.2 Series 4000 Grade 1, lever handles, 2-3/4 in backset, 1/2 in throw latch with 3/4 in throw at rated doors where required by the door label. Latch bolts and strikes at fire-rated doors shall be listed for the door's rating.
B. Functions, by ANSI/BHMA designation used in the sets:
   1. **Classroom security, F110:** deadlocking latch; outside lever locked or unlocked by key from either side; inside lever always retracts the latch for egress; two cylinders.
   2. **Storeroom, F86:** outside lever always fixed; key outside retracts latch; inside lever always free.
   3. **Passage, F75:** both levers always free.
   4. **Privacy with occupancy indicator, F76 w/ indicator:** inside thumbturn locks outside lever; emergency release outside; indicator on corridor side reads "IN USE / VACANT"; inside lever always free.
C. Classroom function F84 (key outside only; cannot be locked from inside) is NOT used on this project.
D. Finish: 626.

**2.4 EXIT DEVICES AND FIRE EXIT HARDWARE**
A. ANSI/BHMA A156.3 Grade 1, touch bar type, stainless steel, finish 630.
B. At fire-rated openings furnish FIRE EXIT HARDWARE listed and labeled for use on fire doors (basis of design 3300-F series); devices with mechanical dogging are not permitted at rated openings. At non-rated openings furnish panic hardware (3300 series).
C. Surface vertical rod devices at rated pairs: less bottom rod (LBR) with the manufacturer's listed auxiliary fire pin, so the pair remains within its listing.
D. Electric latch retraction (ELR) where scheduled: 24 VDC, with integral request-to-exit signal switch and latch bolt monitoring; power supply by this Section sized for simultaneous retraction of all devices on the supply.
E. Lever trim where scheduled: Grade 1 trim listed with the device; function as scheduled.

**2.5 CYLINDERS AND KEYING**
A. Allerton Guardian restricted keyway, Owner's existing grandmaster system extended. Construction cores during construction; permanent cores installed by the hardware supplier in the Owner's presence at Substantial Completion.
B. Keying schedule shall list every cylinder by door mark, keyway, key set, master/grandmaster level, and key quantities.

**2.6 DOOR CLOSERS**
A. ANSI/BHMA A156.4 Grade 1, cast iron body, full rack and pinion, adjustable spring sizes 1 through 6, adjustable backcheck, delayed action where scheduled, finish 689.
B. Arm type as scheduled (regular arm pull side; parallel arm push side). Hold-open arms are NOT permitted at any fire-rated opening; mechanical hold-open permitted only where the set says so.
C. Furnish a closer at every fire-rated door and at every door the sets list a closer for.

**2.7 ELECTROMAGNETIC HOLDERS**
A. Wall-mounted, 24 VDC, with armature and extension as needed; release on loss of power. Holders shall release on fire-alarm activation through a relay/module provided by 28 46 00; the hardware supplier's wiring diagram shall show the relay and the 24 VDC source (power supply by this Section, or fire-alarm panel auxiliary output where 28 46 00 confirms capacity in writing).

**2.8 ELECTRIFIED ACCESSORIES**
A. Electric power transfer (EPT): concealed, mortised in door and frame, 10-wire, furnished by this Section; frame and door prep by 08 11 13 from the EPT manufacturer's template.
B. Power supplies: 24 VDC regulated, UL 294 listed, with fire-alarm input (dry contact from 28 46 00) that releases the ELR or holders on alarm, and relay outputs for access control panel interface. Furnished by this Section, installed by 26 05 00 in the location shown on E-401 (Electrical Room 106 for Level 1 doors).
C. Door position switches, card readers and request-to-exit devices: furnished and installed by 28 10 00; shown on the hardware supplier's wiring diagrams for coordination.

**2.9 PROTECTION PLATES, STOPS, SILENCERS, WEATHER ITEMS**
A. Kick plates: 10 in high x 2 in less door width (LDW), 0.050 in stainless, 630, beveled four edges, countersunk fasteners. Armor plates: 34 in high x 2 in LDW. Mop plates: 6 in high x 1 in LDW.
B. Wall stops: concave, 630; floor stops only where wall stop is impractical.
C. Silencers: three per single door frame, two per leaf at pairs with stops; none at double-egress pairs.
D. Thresholds: extruded aluminum, mill finish, not more than 1/2 in high, beveled, set in sealant, full width of opening.
E. Weatherstripping: head and jambs, adjustable, silicone insert. Door bottom: surface-mounted sweep at exterior HM doors. Rain drip at head.

**2.10 FINISHES** — 626 locks/cylinders; 630 exit devices, pulls, plates, stops, exterior and kitchen hinges; 652 interior hinges; 689 closers and holders.

## SECTION 01 25 00 — SUBSTITUTION PROCEDURES (excerpt)

"1.3.A: A substitution is any product not named in the Specifications. Substitution requests shall be made on the Substitution Request form (Form 01 25 00-A) within 30 days after Notice to Proceed and before the related product submittal; include a point-by-point comparison. 1.3.C: A product submittal that includes an unnamed manufacturer without an approved substitution request will be rejected for that item; the Contractor shall not represent such a product as 'equal' or 'per spec' on the transmittal. 1.3.D: Substitutions for the Owner's standard keyway are not permitted."
