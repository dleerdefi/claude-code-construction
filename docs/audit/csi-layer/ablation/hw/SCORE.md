## (c) Judgment

Both reviews caught all fourteen planted defects with the right governing sources and usable actions, left the Maryland classroom-locking question open, cited nothing from memory, and fabricated nothing, so the choice turns on what sits around the findings. B is the one a PE would rather receive, by a small margin: it puts the gc on the misrepresented transmittal (F-19) as the KEY does, its F-20 catch on the missing 7T-IC chassis and "-34" rated latch suffix is a genuine fabrication-affecting item A did not see, and its routing table is one row per real trade with the 10 Oct 2026 plant date tied to each prep item. A's findings are equally sound but its periphery is softer: F-21 asks for a sequence-of-operation narrative the spec does not require, its routing adds airflow, concrete, flooring and signage rows that are "no" or marginal for a hardware package, and its Section 4 spends rows on storm shelters, wind-borne debris and energy code that no hardware reviewer needs. Both reviews carry one over-raised design-team RFI (A F-22, B F-26) that 2.8.B already answers, and both miss the trade and milestone for the unscheduled D-116 and the threshold's sill-pour dependency. B's 231-row ledger of "Matches the set and Part 2" is bulk a PE would skip, but it is a lesser sin than A's 37.5% na/unverifiable share and off-topic compliance questions, which make A read as a generic Division 08 checklist run rather than a review of this package.
