## PRODUCT DATA

## Pages 25–29 — Stanwick SB series hinges [summarized]
Catalog pages for SB-4545 (4-1/2 x 4-1/2, 0.134 in leaf, standard weight ball bearing, Grade 1; "recommended for doors up to 3'-6" wide, 7'-6" high, 150 lb, medium frequency") and SB-5045-HW (5 x 4-1/2, 0.180 in leaf, heavy weight, "for doors over 3'-6" wide, over 7'-6" high, over 150 lb, high frequency or 90-minute openings"). NRP option. Finishes 652, 630.

## Pages 30–45 — Harbinger Lock Works 7T Series, Grade 1 cylindrical [summarized]
Cover page: "7T Series — ANSI/BHMA A156.2 Series 4000 Grade 1 certified. Lever designs: Tulane, Avon." Function pages (p. 33–38) show 7T-70 Classroom F84 ("key in outside lever locks or unlocks outside lever; inside lever always free; no inside cylinder"), 7T-72 Classroom Security F110 ("key in either lever locks or unlocks outside lever"), 7T-80 Storeroom F86, 7T-40 Privacy F76 ("push-button inside; emergency release outside"; a separate page 37 lists option "-OI occupancy indicator" as an add-on not shown in the schedule), 7T-10 Passage F75. Cylinder page (p. 40): "Accepts Allerton Guardian and other 6-pin interchangeable-core cylinders with 7T-IC chassis." Latch page (p. 41): 1/2 in throw standard; 3/4 in throw "-34" option; UL listed for 3-hour A-label doors with 3/4 in latch. Finishes: 626, 630, 605. Highlighted on page 30 by MADH: "Equal to Allerton 9K in all respects." No point-by-point comparison included.

## Pages 46–49 — Allerton Guardian cylinders [summarized]
Restricted keyway, 6-pin interchangeable core, patent-controlled key blanks, "Chesapeake County Public Schools system — authorized dealer MADH." Construction core program described.

## Pages 50–61 — Northgate 3300 Series exit devices [summarized]
p. 50 cover: "3300 Series — Grade 1 touch bar exit devices. 3301 rim panic; 3327 surface vertical rod; 3300-F fire exit hardware versions for use on fire doors."
p. 52 (reproduced below): **"IMPORTANT — Panic hardware (3301, 3327) is UL 305 listed for use on non-rated doors only. For fire door assemblies specify the -F fire exit hardware version (3301-F, 3327-F), UL 10C listed. Fire exit hardware is not supplied with dogging. LBR fire exit hardware (3327-F-LBR) requires the Model FP-1 auxiliary fire pin on pairs to maintain the pair's listing; the FP-1 is ordered separately."**
p. 54–55: 3301-ELR electric latch retraction: 24 VDC, 16 A inrush / 0.3 A holding; requires a Northgate-approved power supply with latch-retraction output (Voltrex PS-904-ELR or equal); "RX" request-to-exit switch; "LX" latch bolt monitor; wiring to EPT, 10-wire.
p. 56–58: 3301-LT lever trim, functions: passage (always active), classroom (key locks/unlocks), storeroom (always locked). p. 58 note: "Cylinder dogging ('-CD') is a panic-hardware option; not available on -F devices."
p. 59–61: templates and installation dimensions.

## Pages 62–71 — Bancroft 7500 closers [summarized]
Grade 1, cast iron, rack and pinion, adjustable size 1–6, backcheck standard, delayed action option "-DA", hold-open arms "-HO" (page 66 note: "not for fire doors"). RA regular arm, PA parallel arm, HD heavy-duty forged arm. Finish 689. Ten-year warranty statement, page 71.

## Pages 72–75 — Holdfast FM-998 electromagnetic holders [summarized]
Wall-mounted, 24 VDC 0.08 A, 25 lb holding force, armature with 1 in and 3 in extensions, fail-safe release. Page 74 wiring note: "Connect to a 24 VDC source controlled by the fire alarm system through a listed relay. Holder draws 0.08 A continuous."

## Pages 76–80 — Voltrex EPT-10 electric power transfer [summarized]
Concealed, 10-conductor, mortised in hinge-side door edge and frame rabbet; template drawing EPT-10-T (page 78) with mortise dimensions; "frame and door preparation per this template by the door and frame manufacturers."

## Pages 81–84 — Voltrex PS-904 power supply [summarized — included "for reference only; not furnished by MADH"]
24 VDC 4 A regulated, UL 294, fire alarm input (NC dry contact drops output), two Form C relay outputs for ACP interface, 120 VAC input, enclosure 12 x 12 x 4 in. Page 84 ELR version PS-904-ELR with latch-retraction inrush handling for up to 2 devices.

## Pages 85–95 — Beacon trim: kick plates, armor plates, mop plates, stops, silencers, pulls [summarized]
KP-050 kick plates available 8, 10, 12 in heights. OP-10 offset pull; OP-10-CYL with rim cylinder housing.
