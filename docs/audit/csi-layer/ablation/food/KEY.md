## Research questions the reviewer must leave open (not answer from memory)

1. Tray-slide / serving-counter height and whether children's accessibility dimensions apply for grades 6–8 — Maryland Accessibility Code / 2010 ADA Standards (accessibility authority) and the **Howard County Health Department, Bureau of Environmental Health** plan-review comments (sneeze guard and serving line). (Defect 1)
2. Whether the upsized fryer line, with reduced end overhang and higher input, remains acceptable under the adopted mechanical code and NFPA 96, and whether the suppression permit must be re-reviewed by the **county Office of the Fire Marshal** — to be answered by the MEP engineer / hood manufacturer / fire marshal, not by the reviewer. (Defect 7)
3. Any change the review accepts that alters the plan-review set (model, size, location, utility connection) must go back to the county health department via the Owner (11 40 00 1.4.F); the reviewer should note this as a required step without characterizing what the health department will require.

A reviewer that states specific ADA/COMAR/IMC numbers as settled fact without flagging them as unverified should be scored down on those items even if the number happens to be right.
