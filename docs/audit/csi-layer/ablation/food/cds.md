## Division 01 Excerpts

### 01 25 00 — Substitution Procedures

1.3 SUBSTITUTION REQUESTS
A. Submit substitution requests on Form 01 25 00-A within 30 days after Notice to Proceed. Requests received after that date will be considered only for products that have become unavailable through no fault of the Contractor, with documentation from the manufacturer.
B. Where a specification section names a product as "Basis of Design" with the phrase "or approved equal" or "or comparable product," that phrase does not waive this Article. A product of another manufacturer is a substitution and requires an approved Form 01 25 00-A before it appears in a submittal.
C. Submittals that show a manufacturer or model other than the Basis of Design without an approved substitution request will be returned "Revise and Resubmit" without technical review of that item.
D. A different model from the same manufacturer, with different capacity, dimensions, or utility requirements, is a deviation and shall be identified as such on the transmittal with the reason for the deviation.

### 01 33 00 — Submittal Procedures (excerpt)

1.5 PRODUCT DATA
B. Mark each copy to show the applicable model, options, finishes, and accessories. Delete or strike inapplicable data. Where a catalog or multi-model data sheet is submitted, the applicable product shall be clearly identified; unmarked catalogs will be returned without review.
C. Where a Section includes an equipment schedule, the submittal shall address every scheduled item. A partial package for a scheduled section will be returned as incomplete unless the transmittal states which items are deferred and why.

1.8 DEVIATIONS
A. Identify deviations from the Contract Documents on the transmittal. Approval of a submittal does not relieve the Contractor of conformance with the Contract Documents for deviations not identified.

## Section 11 40 00 — Foodservice Equipment (excerpts)

### PART 1 — GENERAL

1.2 SUMMARY
A. Section includes furnishing and installing the foodservice equipment items tagged on the FS series drawings and scheduled on FS-201, including custom-fabricated stainless steel items, walk-in cooler/freezer and refrigeration, cooking equipment, serving counters, warewashing equipment, and shelving.
B. Related work: Type I exhaust hood KH-1 and its fire-suppression system — Section 23 38 13. Floor sinks, floor troughs, indirect waste piping, grease interceptor, water, and gas piping to point of connection — Division 22. Branch circuits, disconnects and receptacles — Division 26. Wall backing — Section 06 10 00. Slab depression — Division 03 / S-sheets.

1.4 SUBMITTALS
A. Product data: manufacturer's data for each scheduled item, marked per 01 33 00 to show the model, voltage/phase, options and accessories being furnished. Include NSF/ANSI listing and UL/ETL listing for each item.
B. Shop drawings: 1/4" = 1'-0" minimum plan showing all items in place with tags; rough-in drawings at 1/4" scale showing every electrical, plumbing and gas point of connection with height AFF, size, voltage/phase/amps, and connection type (cord-and-plug with NEMA configuration, or direct); custom fabrication drawings at 3/4" = 1'-0" with gauge, finish, and construction details.
C. Utility summary: tabulated schedule of every item's utility requirements as submitted, in the same format as FS-201, with any difference from FS-201 identified.
D. Wall backing drawing: plan and elevation locating every wall-mounted or wall-supported item (shelving, hand sinks, wall-mounted tables, wall brackets) with the extent (length, bottom and top AFF) of backing required, for use by Section 06 10 00. Submit no later than the first 11 40 00 submittal; backing is installed before wall close-in.
E. Hood/appliance coordination: an elevation of the cooking line under hood KH-1 showing each appliance's width, depth, height, and gas/electric input, dimensioned to the hood ends and to the hood front, for comparison with the M-401 hood schedule. Report any difference from the M-401 appliance design basis in writing to the Architect before equipment is released for fabrication.
F. Health department plan review: the Owner's foodservice consultant prepared the plan-review drawings submitted to the county health department before bid. Where any item submitted under this Section differs from the FS drawings in model, size, location, or utility connection, the KEC shall furnish product data for that item to the Owner so the plan-review file can be updated. The KEC shall not assume that a change is acceptable to the health department.

A. Custom fabricated items: NSF/ANSI 2 certified fabricator.
B. Electrical equipment: listed and labeled by a nationally recognized testing laboratory.
C. Walk-in: NSF/ANSI 7; panels UL 723 flame spread 25 or less.

Verify that the voltage, phase, ampacity, and connection type of each item as submitted match the branch circuit scheduled on E-601; report any difference in writing before Division 26 orders breakers or pulls feeders.
B. Verify that the walk-in box as submitted fits the slab depression on S-101 and the clearances on FS-101. Report any difference to the Architect before the slab is formed.
C. Verify that each indirect waste as submitted terminates at a floor sink or floor trough shown on P-101. Report any indirect waste with no receptor shown.
D. Verify the cooking line against the M-401 hood schedule (1.4.E).

1.7 DELIVERY
A. Walk-in panels, condensing units and large fabricated items: coordinate delivery with the GC's schedule for the slab depression and roof curbs.

A. Manufacturers and models scheduled on FS-201 are the Basis of Design. Products of other manufacturers are subject to 01 25 00.

A. Tops, bowls, drainboards, and backsplashes of sinks, work tables, and dish tables: 14-gauge Type 304 stainless steel, No. 4 finish. Undershelves, legs and cross bracing: 16-gauge Type 304 (legs 1-5/8" OD tubular, 16-gauge). Bullet feet, adjustable, stainless.
B. Sink bowls: coved corners 3/4" radius, integrally welded, with 1-1/2" basket-strainer waste outlet. Hand sinks: as scheduled (manufactured, not custom).

Insulated floor panels in both compartments, 4" thick, with 1/8" aluminum treadplate wear floor, set in the 4" slab depression shown on S-101 so the finished walk-in floor is flush with the kitchen floor. Interior wall-to-wall between cooler and freezer: 4" insulated partition.
B. Doors: 36" x 78" each compartment, self-closing, with heated frame (freezer), interior safety release, 14" x 24" vision panel, thermometer with alarm, LED interior lighting.

D. Refrigeration: remote air-cooled condensing units on roof curbs (curbs by Division 07), piped to ceiling-mounted unit coolers; R-448A or R-449A; electric defrost at freezer; condensate drains with heat trace in freezer, run to floor sink FS-E outside the box.
E. Electrical: condensing units 208V/3-phase/60 Hz as scheduled on FS-201 and E-601. Unit coolers 120V/1-phase, fed from the condensing unit control circuit.

2.4 COOKING EQUIPMENT
A. Range FS-12, fryer battery FS-13, convection oven FS-14: natural gas, 7" w.c. inlet, with 3/4" NPT quick-disconnect gas hoses and restraining cables, listed to the applicable ANSI Z83 standard. Inputs as scheduled on FS-201; the M-401 hood schedule is based on these inputs and widths.
B. Fryers: 45-50 lb oil capacity each, built-in filtration system on the battery, millivolt controls.

2.5 SERVING COUNTERS (FS-18, FS-19)
A. Custom modular serving counters, stainless steel body and top, with tray slide per 2.2.D and full-service sneeze guard with glass top and front panel, LED display lighting, and heat lamps at FS-18. Counter body top 36" AFF; tray slide 34" AFF as shown on A-401 (see A-401 note regarding verification).
B. Hot food wells: 3 wells, 12" x 20" each, drains manifolded to one 1" indirect waste.
C. Cold pan: 4-pan mechanically refrigerated cold pan, self-contained, NSF 7; condensate to indirect waste.

2.6 WAREWASHING (FS-21, FS-22)
A. Dishwasher: door-type, hot water sanitizing, straight-through, with factory-mounted electric booster heater sized for a 70°F rise at the inlet temperature shown on P-101, 208V/3-phase. Chemical sanitizing machines are not acceptable.
B. Dish tables: per 2.2; soiled table with scrap sink, pre-rinse assembly, and rack shelf; clean table with 3-sided rolled edge.

2.7 SHELVING (FS-11)
A. Stainless steel wire wall shelving, NSF, 18" deep, in runs scheduled on FS-201 and located on A-401 and FS-101. Wall brackets with stainless fasteners; anchors appropriate to the wall construction at each location (metal stud with wood backing, or CMU).

2.8 ICE MACHINE (FS-6)
A. Air-cooled cuber on storage bin, with inline water filter assembly; indirect drain.

### PART 3 — EXECUTION

3.1 INSTALLATION
A. Set equipment level; seal fixed equipment to walls and floor with NSF silicone; 6" minimum legs on floor-mounted equipment; close gaps between adjoining fixed equipment with stainless trim strips.
B. Make final connections to the points of connection provided by Divisions 22 and 26 (cord-and-plug connections by this Section; direct connections by Divisions 22 and 26).
C. Walk-in: install floor panels in the depression on a leveling bed; after installation, Division 03 fills the perimeter gap with non-shrink grout. Install roof-mounted condensing units on curbs by Division 07.
D. Wall-mounted items: anchor to backing or masonry per the approved backing drawing. Do not anchor to gypsum board or metal stud alone.
3.3 START-UP AND TRAINING
A. Manufacturer's start-up for walk-in refrigeration, dishwasher, and cooking equipment; 4 hours of staff training; O&M manuals.
