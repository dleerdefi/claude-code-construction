## (c) Judgment

A PE would rather receive review A. Both reviews catch the same eleven defects and miss none, both partial on the same two items for the same owner reasons, and both reach the right disposition, so the difference is in what surrounds the hits: A carries one false finding against B's two, and A's F-21 turns the energy-code edition question into an actionable finding with a GC owner and a research step where B leaves it as an unverifiable ledger row and a question. A's ledger is 122 rows with one unverifiable and zero "na", and its routing table has a single "not relevant" row, whereas nearly a fifth of B's ledger and routing rows are "na"/"unverifiable"/"no" entries (wood trusses, metal buildings, foodservice, housekeeping pads) that a PE must read past. B's hook-driven compliance list (fourteen questions, including classroom acoustics and energy recovery) and its sound-data and condensate findings show the knowledge layer generating requirements the documents do not contain, which is exactly the habit the KEY's bait items test for. B does have real edges — its F-03 enumerates every deviation by tag, F-10 names the science-lab stake, F-11 asks for itemized option weights, and it attaches a milestone to the warranty item — but A's tighter package, correct "by others" mapping, and single low-severity false item would cost the PE less time to redline.
