## S-001 — Structural General Notes (excerpt)

Design loads, roof: dead 20 psf (including 3 psf mechanical allowance distributed), live 20 psf, snow (flat roof) 25 psf, drift per code.
Wind: ultimate design wind speed V_ult = 115 mph, Exposure B, Risk Category III. Components and cladding pressures for rooftop equipment per S-001 Table 3.
Seismic: Site Class D, Seismic Design Category B, Component importance factor I_p = 1.0 for HVAC equipment not required for life safety.
Rooftop equipment attachment to curbs and curbs to structure shall be designed by the equipment supplier for the loads above; calculations signed and sealed by a professional engineer licensed in the State of Maryland (see Specification 23 74 13 and 23 05 48).

## SECTION 01 25 00 — SUBSTITUTION PROCEDURES (excerpt)

1.3 SUBSTITUTIONS AFTER AWARD
A. Products named in the Contract Documents as "basis of design" establish the standard of quality, performance, dimensions, weight, utilities and appearance. Products listed as "acceptable manufacturers" in a specification section are pre-qualified only as to manufacturer; the specific product submitted must comply with every requirement of the section and the schedules.
B. A product by any manufacturer not named in a specification section is a substitution. Submit Substitution Request Form 01 25 00-A, separately from and before the related product submittal, including: reason for substitution, point-by-point comparison with the basis of design, effect on other trades (structural, electrical, plumbing, controls, roofing), effect on schedule, and cost change. The Architect will accept or reject within 14 days.
C. A product submittal that includes an unnamed manufacturer without an accepted substitution request will be returned "Rejected – Resubmit" without technical review of that item.
D. Submittals shall identify every deviation from the Contract Documents on the transmittal. Deviations not identified on the transmittal are not accepted even if the submittal is stamped "No Exceptions Taken."

## SECTION 23 74 13 — PACKAGED OUTDOOR CENTRAL-STATION AIR-HANDLING UNITS (excerpts)

### PART 1 — GENERAL

1.2 SUMMARY
A. Section includes packaged rooftop units with DX cooling and gas-fired heating, factory-mounted controls, roof curbs and accessories, tags RTU-1 through RTU-6 as scheduled on M-601.

1.3 ACTION SUBMITTALS
A. Product Data: For each unit scheduled, include manufacturer, model, AHRI-certified capacities at the scheduled entering and ambient conditions, supply fan performance at the scheduled CFM and external static pressure, electrical data (voltage, phase, MCA, MOCP), efficiency ratings (EER, IEER, heating efficiency), sound power data, filter data, economizer and relief data, and all options and accessories included.
B. Shop Drawings: Dimensional drawings; operating weight of each unit including curb and all factory-installed options; corner weights; roof curb drawings with curb height and relationship to the roof assembly on A-511; clearance diagrams; duct connection sizes and locations; gas and electrical connection locations; wiring diagrams; controls interface drawings.
C. Seismic and Wind Restraint Submittals: Calculations and attachment details for unit-to-curb and curb-to-structure fastening for the wind and seismic loads on S-001, signed and sealed by a professional engineer licensed in Maryland. Submit with the product data; the product submittal is incomplete without them.
D. Deviations: List every deviation from this section and from the M-601 schedule on the transmittal, with the reason for each.

1.4 INFORMATIONAL AND CLOSEOUT SUBMITTALS
A. Start-up reports (closeout): submit within 14 days of start-up of each unit.
B. Field quality-control reports; warranty documents; operation and maintenance data.

1.5 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. Capacities certified under AHRI Standard 340/360. Units listed to UL 1995 or UL 60335-2-40. Gas furnaces certified to ANSI Z21.47 / CSA 2.3.
B. Energy efficiency: minimum cooling and heating efficiencies shall comply with the energy code adopted by the Authority Having Jurisdiction, as summarized on G-002, for each unit's capacity class. Where a scheduled efficiency exceeds the code minimum, the scheduled value governs.
C. Ventilation: outdoor air quantities as scheduled on M-601 are design minimums derived from ASHRAE 62.1; do not reduce.

1.6 WARRANTY
A. Manufacturer's standard warranty: 1 year parts and labor from Substantial Completion.
B. Compressors: 5 years parts, from Substantial Completion, included in the Contract Sum.
C. Gas heat exchangers: 10 years parts, included in the Contract Sum.

1.7 START-UP AND COMMISSIONING
A. Start-up by a factory-authorized service representative using the manufacturer's start-up checklist. Give the Engineer and Commissioning Authority 72 hours' notice.
B. Start-up shall include refrigerant charge verification by subcooling/superheat, combustion analysis of each gas furnace with results recorded, verification of economizer operation and minimum outdoor-air damper position at the scheduled OA CFM (coordinate with Section 23 05 93 TAB), verification of smoke detector shutdown, and verification of BACnet communication with the BAS (Section 23 09 00).
C. Do not start units for temporary heating or cooling without written Owner approval; filters shall be replaced before Substantial Completion.

### PART 2 — PRODUCTS

2.1 MANUFACTURERS
A. Basis of Design: Carrier Corporation, WeatherMaker 48LC series, with options as scheduled.
B. Acceptable Manufacturers, subject to compliance with this section and the M-601 schedule: Trane (Precedent / Voyager); Daikin Applied (Rebel / Maverick); Johnson Controls / York (Sun Premier / Predator).
C. Products of other manufacturers: subject to Section 01 25 00.

2.2 UNIT DESCRIPTION
A. Factory-assembled, single-piece packaged rooftop unit with DX cooling, natural-gas heating, supply fan, economizer with barometric relief, filters, controls and roof curb.
B. Capacities scheduled on M-601 are minimums at the scheduled conditions. Certified capacities within 3 percent below the scheduled value are acceptable; capacities more than 3 percent below are a deviation.
C. Supply fan: direct-drive with VFD or ECM motor, selected to deliver the scheduled CFM at the scheduled external static pressure with filters loaded to 0.5 in. w.g.
D. Compressors: scroll, minimum two stages of cooling on units 10 tons and larger; variable or multi-stage on RTU-5.
E. Casing: double-wall, foam-insulated, hinged access doors on filter, fan and control compartments; hail guards on condenser coils.

2.3 FILTERS
A. 4-inch deep pleated filters, MERV 13 per ASHRAE 52.2, in a factory rack with gasketed tracks and hinged access door, for all units unless otherwise scheduled.

2.4 ECONOMIZER AND OUTDOOR AIR
A. Integrated outdoor-air economizer with low-leakage dampers, differential enthalpy or dry-bulb changeover as scheduled, barometric relief, and a minimum outdoor-air damper position factory-set and field-verified to deliver the scheduled outdoor-air CFM.
B. Economizer fault detection and diagnostics (FDD) as required by the adopted energy code.
C. RTU-5: CO2 demand-controlled ventilation with space sensor by Section 23 09 00.

2.5 GAS HEATING
A. Tubular heat exchanger, stainless steel; induced-draft; direct spark ignition; two-stage or modulating; input as scheduled on M-601.
B. Natural gas supply at 7 in. w.c. at the unit inlet; line-pressure regulators at each unit by Division 22 per P-501. Gas connection size and location per shop drawings.

2.6 ELECTRICAL
A. Single-point power connection at the voltage scheduled; factory-mounted non-fused disconnect; phase monitor; 115 V GFCI convenience outlet powered from a factory transformer.
B. MCA and MOCP shall not exceed the values scheduled on M-601, which are coordinated with the branch circuits on E-601. Units with options that increase MCA above the scheduled value shall be identified as a deviation.

2.7 CONTROLS
A. Factory-mounted DDC unit controller communicating with the building automation system by BACnet MS/TP, providing the points in Section 23 09 00 Table 23 09 00-A at minimum.
B. Factory-provided terminals for duct smoke detector shutdown (supply and return) wired to the unit controller to shut down the supply fan and close outdoor-air dampers on alarm.
C. Dirty filter differential-pressure switch, factory-mounted across the filter bank, reported to the BAS.

2.8 ROOF CURBS
A. Full-perimeter, 14-gauge galvanized steel, fully insulated, continuous wood nailer, factory-supplied by the unit manufacturer, shipped knocked-down for field assembly.
B. Curb height shall suit the roof assembly on A-511 so that the top of curb is at the height above the finished roof membrane required by A-511 at each unit location.

### PART 3 — EXECUTION (excerpt)

3.2 INSTALLATION
A. Set units on curbs level and secured per the sealed restraint submittal. Install duct smoke detectors furnished under Division 28 in the unit or duct per the detector manufacturer's instructions and the fire alarm drawings; see FA-001 note 12.
B. Units exceeding the allowable operating weight at their location on S-201 shall not be set until supplemental steel is in place.
