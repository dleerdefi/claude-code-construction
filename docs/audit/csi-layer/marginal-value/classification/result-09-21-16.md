## 3. Best (b) items

1. **09gb.rh.rated-wall-continuity** — "For each kind of rated wall on this project (fire wall, fire barrier, shaft enclosure, fire partition, smoke barrier, smoke partition), must it run to the floor or roof deck above under the adopted code, or may it stop at a rated ceiling membrane, and what has to continue above the ceiling or through concealed spaces?" — The baseline asks what hourly rating each wall carries but never whether it may terminate at a rated ceiling; in a K-12 that decision sets which corridor walls go to deck, which drives the firestop, HVAC transfer and framing scope.
2. **09gb.rc.acoustic-separations** — "Each room adjacency with a sound criterion in the acoustic report or spec is separated by a partition type whose tested rating meets it, that runs to structure or stops at a ceiling the acoustic design accepted, and whose doors, transfer openings and ducts are accounted for." — Baseline overlays the Life Safety plan for fire but has no equivalent overlay for acoustic adjacencies; it checks the STC report against the schedule, not the schedule against the rooms.
3. **if.09-plenum-return-walls** — "Partitions running to structure across a ceiling return plenum (rated, acoustic, security), and framed openings where transfers pass through them ... Return-air transfer ducts or openings through those walls, with fire or smoke dampers in rated walls and lined or offset transfers in sound-rated walls" — Baseline routes damper sleeves but never return-air transfers; cutting transfers into rated/STC walls after boarding is a frequent K-12 field defect.
4. **09gb.recessed-in-rated** — "Items recessed into rated walls (fire extinguisher cabinets, recessed toilet accessories, medicine cabinets, panelboards, access doors, dryer and valve boxes) are rated products or are backed by construction that keeps the wall's rating, fit the stud depth, and have their rough openings framed as the listed design allows." — Baseline only covers rated plumbing access panels; non-rated FECs in rated corridors are a standard inspection failure.
5. **09gb.access-doors** — "Every concealed item behind gypsum walls and above gypsum ceilings that needs service or inspection (valves, fire and smoke dampers, terminal units, cleanouts, trap primers, junction boxes, controllers) has an access door on the RCP or elevations, sized for the work, rated where the wall or ceiling is rated, and framed before board." — Baseline asks plumbing for access-panel locations but has no check of mechanical/electrical devices above gypsum ceilings and soffits.
6. **if.09-partition-plenum-barrier** — "Sound barrier from the partition track or head to the structure, built to the rating the acoustic design needs ... Confirm who: Sound barrier above the partition track — typical furnish 09 21 16 / install 09 21 16" — Operable partitions are routine in K-12 and the barrier above the track is the classic unowned scope; baseline is silent on 10 22 00.

## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items COMPILED lacks entirely)

1. Type X vs Type C distinction — Type C required by many ceiling-membrane and shaft-wall designs; not interchangeable (baseline #3, #17, #58).
2. Fastener length per layer, minimum penetration into steel, drill-point (C954) screws at 16 ga and heavier framing (baseline #5, #19, #59, #75).
3. Shaft wall listing tested from one side only vs both-side exposure, and hoistway lateral pressure at the specified deflection (baseline #18, #50).
4. STC report must be a lab test of the same stud spacing/gauge/insulation/channel, not a calculated value; 24" o.c. test cannot justify a 16" o.c. wall (baseline #11, #24).
5. Accessory material by exposure — zinc/vinyl bead and control joints at wet and exterior-wall interior faces; galvanized bead rust bleed (baseline #6, #68).
6. Ceiling board thickness vs framing spacing and sag-resistant board (baseline #22).
7. Seismic bracing of partitions and shaft walls under ASCE 7 Ch. 13 for Risk Category III schools (baseline #52, #83).
8. ANSI/ASA S12.60 classroom acoustic minimums (STC 50 classroom-corridor, 55 to music/mechanical) as the K-12 criterion source (baseline #53, #80, #82).
9. Board storage and mold before installation; Buy American / domestic steel on publicly funded schools; ASME A17.1 hoistway projection bevels (baseline #64, #56, #55).
10. Standards missing from the compiled list: ASTM C1002/C954 (screws), C475 (joint compound/tape), C1047 (accessories), C1177/C1178 (glass-mat sheathing/backer), E119/UL 263, UL 2079, E84/UL 723, AISI S220 (baseline #72, #75, #76, #78, #79, #81).

## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) dominate at 94%: the compiled layer re-derives what an expert baseline already listed, pads it with Division 01/09 boilerplate and off-section flooring and schedule items, and its failure modes largely restate its own checks. Its six genuine contributions are real (rated-wall continuity, acoustic adjacency overlay, return-air transfers, recessed items in rated walls, access doors above hard ceilings, operable-partition barrier), but the baseline holds more section-specific knowledge the layer lacks (Type C, fastener penetration, one-sided shaft listings, STC test matching, accessory corrosion, the fastener/joint/accessory ASTMs).
