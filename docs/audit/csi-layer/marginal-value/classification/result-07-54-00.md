## 3. Best (b) items

1. **rf.leak-detection-ready** — "Where electronic leak detection is specified, the method suits the membrane and deck (a conductive deck, or a conductive layer under the membrane, since some methods cannot test conductive membranes), and any conductive layer is installed with the roofing."
   Why: the baseline names ASTM D7877 and stops. Over polyiso and cover board the membrane has no conductive substrate; if the conductive primer/mesh is not in the submitted assembly, the specified ELD cannot be performed at closeout and cannot be retrofitted. A reviewer would need to add the conductive layer to the component schedule now.

2. **if.roof-edge-nailers** — "Send them: Nailer size, height and fastening that the edge system and its test require. Need from them: Nailers installed and fastened to that design before edge metal and copings. If missed: Edge metal fastened to undersized or under-fastened nailers; roof edge lifts in high wind."
   Why: the baseline's edge-metal check (#25, #31, #68) stays inside Division 07; nobody sends the nailer fastening schedule that the ES-1 listing and FM 1-49 assume to the carpentry sub, and the GC installs the blocking before the edge submittal arrives.

3. **rf.temporary-roof** — "If the roof will dry in the building early (temporary roofing, phased areas, permanent membrane used during construction), the spec's position and the manufacturer's warranty conditions for it are confirmed."
   Why: on a summer-window school job the GC routinely uses the permanent membrane as the dry-in; manufacturers condition the NDL warranty on protection and a pre-warranty inspection of that use. The baseline covers trade damage (#66) but never asks the warranty question.

4. **edu.storm-shelter** — "Where the school has a storm shelter or safe room, every element of the shelter envelope (walls, roof deck and covering ...) is submitted with evidence that it meets ICC 500 pressure and debris-impact testing as the tested assembly ..."
   Why: the baseline would raise the risk category and wind pressures for a shelter but not require the roof over the shelter to be submitted as part of the ICC 500 tested assembly. Only the roof-envelope part is relevant to this section; the door/louver content is not.

5. **rf.rh.rooftop-guards** — "Does the adopted code require guards or fall protection at roof hatches and at equipment near the roof edge?"
   Why: the baseline coordinates anchor posts that are already designed (#34) but never asks whether guards are required at the hatch and at equipment within 10 ft of the edge; when they are added late, their posts become unplanned membrane penetrations.

## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items COMPILED lacks entirely)

1. Fastener length vs insulation stack at the thickest point of the taper plus required deck penetration (#20, #46, #59) — the single most common securement defect on tapered systems.
2. Base flashing 8" minimum above finished membrane after taper build-up at every wall and curb (#21, #24, #44, #63) — compiled mentions base flashing height only in the skylight route.
3. ASCE 7 edition and the different zone geometry of 7-16/7-22 vs 7-10 for the perimeter/corner widths (#19, #60).
4. Membrane thickness downgrade (60 → 45 mil) and thickness over scrim on the data sheet (#1, #13).
5. Seam welding parameters, factory-seam sample and the cold/scorched-weld failure mode (#12, #57) — the signature TPO/PVC failure and the installation manual pages that go with it.
6. Insulation R-value compliance path: average R vs R at the drain sump on a tapered system (#17, #48).
7. Cool-roof CRRC aged reflectance/SRI and non-white membrane acceptance (#1, #26, #49, #78) — the energy hook mentions reflectance only in passing.
8. State product approvals (Florida Product Approval, Miami-Dade NOA, TDI) and VOC rules for solvent-based adhesives (#53, #55, #85).
9. Insurer requirements beyond the spec: FM 1-60/1-90 and FM 4473 severe-hail class (#54, #82).
10. The section's product standards: ASTM D6878, D4434/D6754, C1289 polyiso, C1177/C1278 cover boards, E108/UL 790, UL 1256/FM 4450, SPRI FX-1 pull-out, D7877/D5957 (#70–79) — compiled lists only two standards for a thermoplastic membrane section.
