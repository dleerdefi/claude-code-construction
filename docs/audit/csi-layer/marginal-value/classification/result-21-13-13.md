## 3. Best (b) items

1. **21wb.component-pressure** — "Where static pressure, fire pump churn or fire department pumping through the connection can exceed standard component ratings, the submittal shows high-pressure rated pipe, fittings, valves and sprinklers or pressure-reducing valves, and the riser diagram shows the pressure zones the design requires." The baseline checks pipe standards, velocity and C-factors but never asks whether 175 psi components survive FDC pumping or a high-static site; a reviewer would default from memory.

2. **d21.existing-system-work** — "Where new work extends or modifies an existing system, the existing piping, water supply and hazard are re-evaluated (new heads on an old system are calculated with the old pipe and a current supply), and each shutdown follows the owner's impairment procedure with notice to the fire department, the monitoring company and the insurer." The baseline is written for new construction only; on a school addition tied into an existing riser this is the first thing that goes wrong.

3. **if.08-sprinklered-glazing** — "Need from them: Where the design protects ordinary glazing with sprinklers instead of fire-rated glazing, glass type and size, and framing and window treatments kept out of the zone between sprinklers and glass. If missed: Blinds, deep mullions or a different glass type installed where sprinklers protect the glazing; the protection no longer matches its listing." Window sprinklers are listed as an assembly; nothing in the baseline would make a reviewer pull 08 80 00 to find out whether the design relies on them.

4. **if.11-stage-fly-space** — "Need from them: Batten travel envelopes, loft wells, curtain tracks and hoist locations. If missed: Sprinkler mains, ducts or conduit routed through batten travel or loft wells." Baseline treats the stage as an OH2 hazard pocket and a coverage area, not as a rigging envelope that mains must stay out of.

5. **if.13-isolation-crossings** — "Send them: Flexible connections, resilient hangers and sleeves at every crossing of isolated floors, walls and ceilings. If missed: A rigid conduit, pipe, sprinkler drop or screw bridges the isolation." Band and music rooms on schools are frequently box-in-box; a rigid drop bridging the isolation is a classic acoustic defect no sprinkler reviewer looks for unprompted.

## 4. Every (c) item

1. **if.21-release-control** — "→ Fire detection and alarm (28 46 00) · Gate: procurement_release — The panel and the valve actuators must be listed together. Send them: Release valve or actuator models, the releasing panels they are listed with, and the release logic (single or double interlock, cross-zoned detection, time delay and abort) ... Confirm who: Releasing control panel and its detection — typical furnish 21 13 00, 21 22 00 or 28 46 00 ... If missed: The releasing panel and the agent valve actuators are not listed together." A wet-pipe system has no releasing panel, no interlock logic and no abort switch. ## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items the compiled context lacks entirely)

1. **Seismic bracing package** (baseline #9, #48, #76): brace locations, NFPA 13 Ch. 18 loads, listed brace components, flexible couplings at the riser and building seismic joints, SDC/Ip from structural general notes. Compiled has only the generic "importance factor matches structural notes" line and never mentions sway bracing at all — on a Risk Category III school this is the largest omission.
2. **Hangers and attachments** (#23, #28, #47, #67): spacing by pipe size, rod size, cracked-concrete anchors, powder-actuated limits, no hanging from deck or bridging, EOR acceptance of attachment points. Compiled only routes main loads to the joist supplier.
3. **Pipe, fitting and joining standards** (#10, #18, #21, #22, #90, #91): A53 E/S vs Type F, A795, Sch 10 not threaded, listed grooved couplings, pipe schedule stating what is used where. Compiled asks only for "pipe and fitting with listing and pressure rating marked".
4. **Hydraulic calculation internals** (#17, #18, #19, #42, #45): C-factor and ID entered vs pipe schedule, velocity cap, hose allowance, node diagram reconciled to plan lengths/fittings/elevations. Compiled checks "every remote area" and the supply curve only.
5. **Sprinkler legend ↔ product data reconciliation and spare heads** (#8, #41): SIN/K/temp/response/finish matched line by line, spare-head cabinet quantities.
6. **Coverage and spacing rules** (#15, #24): spacing within listing, beam rule, three-times rule, QR area-reduction validity conditions. Compiled covers obstructions but not coverage area or the QR reduction.
7. **Riser hardware detail** (#26): main drain size, gauges either side of the check valve, inspector's test at the remote point, FDC check valve and ball drip, FDC thread/coupling per department.
8. **Flow switch retard setting and nuisance alarms** (#72).
9. **Welding credentials** (#12): WPS/PQR, welder qualification, fabricator.
10. **Product standards** (#83–#92): UL 199, 213, 203/203A, 262/1091/312/193, 346/753/405, 1821, ASTM, ASME B16, ASCE 7 Ch. 13, ACI 355.2. Compiled lists five standards and none for pipe, hangers, couplings, valves or sprinklers. Also missing from the education overlay: head guards in gyms/locker rooms and institutional heads in corridors (#94).

## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) dominate at 95%: the compiled context restates the baseline for the water supply, hazard, coverage, freezing, elevator, hood, backflow and supervision topics, pads it with Division 01 boilerplate and parent-scope dry/preaction/deluge items that do not belong in a wet-pipe section, and drags in healthcare routings (MRI shielding, cubicle track, escalators) that the education overlay should have suppressed. The five genuine additions (component pressure rating, existing-system tie-ins, sprinklered glazing, stage rigging envelopes, isolation crossings) are real but narrow, while the layer entirely lacks seismic bracing, hangers, pipe standards, calc internals and the product-standard list that the closed-book baseline produced.
