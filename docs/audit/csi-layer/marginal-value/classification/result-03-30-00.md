## 3. Best (b) items

1. **03cip.rc.depressions** — "Every area with a thicker floor assembly (thick-set tile, tile over a waterproofing or crack-isolation membrane, terrazzo, recessed entrance mats, shower floors and sloped beds to floor drains, walk-in coolers) has a structural depression equal to the full assembly depth over its full extent, so the finish meets the adjacent floor flush." — The baseline only names kitchen depressions; this makes the reviewer walk the finish schedule against the structural slab plan and catches the "depth = tile only" error before the pour.
2. **03cip.mass-placements** — "Thick placements (mats, large pile caps, transfer girders) are identified, and where the spec or the element calls for it a thermal control plan covers temperature limits, monitoring and protection." — The baseline has no mass-concrete item; an absence check for the ACI 301 thermal control plan is a real gap.
3. **if.03-cold-storage-floors** — "The floor the approved box needs, settled before forming (a depression for insulated floor panels, or an insulated slab with the vapor retarder on the warm side and underfloor heat below it), finished flush at the door" — Freezer-on-grade underfloor heat and the insulated-slab assembly are routinely missed in K-12 kitchens; nothing in the baseline raises them.
4. **if.03-grounding-electrode** — "Footing reinforcing and a pour hold so the concrete-encased electrode can be installed, bonded and inspected" — A concrete reviewer rarely thinks of the Ufer ground; a missed electrode means a substitute system and an inspection finding.
5. **if.03-pile-caps** — "As-built pile location and cut-off elevation survey, and pile acceptance (load tests, integrity tests, driving or drilling records) before caps are formed" — Where piles exist, this gate (caps adjusted to as-built pile positions) is absent from the baseline, which assumed spread footings.
6. **d03.rh.embodied-carbon** — "Does a state or local law, or an owner program the spec names, cap the global warming potential of concrete or precast products or require product-specific environmental product declarations, and do the submitted mixes and products carry them?" — No baseline item; where it applies the mix submittal is incomplete without EPDs and a reviewer would not ask from memory.
7. **if.03-floor-recesses** — "Blockouts and recesses formed at the pour to the approved product (floor closer and pivot boxes, sliding-door floor tracks, mobile shelving rails, cart-loading sterilizer pits)... products that will be selected late are flagged so the recess is held open or deliberately omitted" — The baseline covers embeds but not recesses tied to late-selected architectural products; mobile shelving in a library and floor closers at entrances are common K-12 cases.
8. **if.03-dock-leveler-pits** — "Pits formed to the approved leveler's pit drawing, curb angles and embed plates anchored as detailed, pit slope or drain, and dock-face embeds for bumpers and restraints" — Where a dock exists, the pit is on the structural set as a generic detail; the baseline has no interface to catch the approved-leveler mismatch.

Compiled has no rebar check, reconciliation, must_show or standard.
2. **f'cr recomputation and strength-record basis** (2, 19): compiled asks for the record but not for recomputing f'c + 1.34s / f'c + 2.33s − 500 or rejecting average-only mixes.
3. **Max aggregate size vs the most congested element** (20).
4. **SCM limits and cold-weather finishability** (21).
5. **Chloride ion summation across admixtures, aggregate and water** (23) and the non-chloride accelerator / single-manufacturer admixture compatibility statement (5).
6. **Mill certificates for cement and SCMs** with actual chemical/physical values (4), and aggregate test data beyond ASR (3).
7. **Shoring/reshoring and formwork drawings** stamped by an engineer, with reshore-removal strength criteria (16, 66); compiled has only generic delegated-design and the decision-without-strength failure mode.
8. **Anchor rod grade/weldability (F1554 S1), headed stud and plate data** (9) and the steel-side F1554/A108/AWS D1.1 standards (92).
9. **Slump at point of placement / separate pump mix targets / pump air loss on exterior flatwork** (24, 84) and **cylinder curing-box / sampling-point discipline** (13, 32, 82).

## 6. Verdict

Yes, (a)+(d) dominate at 90%: the compiled layer restates the baseline's slab, curing, vapor retarder, cast-in and testing checks (a) and pads them with Division 01 boilerplate and K-12 overlay items that do not belong in a concrete package (d). Its marginal value is a handful of cross-trade cast-in/recess gates (freezer floors, Ufer ground, pile as-builts, depressions at full assembly depth) and two absence checks (mass concrete, EPDs), while it drops the baseline's entire materials, reinforcing and formwork engineering review and most of its standards list.
