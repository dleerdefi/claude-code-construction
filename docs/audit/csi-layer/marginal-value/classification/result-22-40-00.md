## 3. Best (b) items

1. **d22.concealed-access** — "Valves, cleanouts, trap primers, water hammer arresters, mixing valves, backflow assemblies and other serviceable devices concealed in walls or above hard ceilings have access panels located on the architectural drawings, sized for the service, and placed where the finishes allow (not in tile or feature walls without the architect's approval)."
   Why it changes a review: the baseline checks that trap primers and ASSE 1070/1017 valves exist and are the right type, but never asks whether they can be reached once the chase wall is tiled. A fixture reviewer following the baseline would approve a chase-mounted trap primer or master mixing valve and only discover at punch that the architect never drew an access panel in the tile wall.

## 4. Every (c) item

1. **edu.risk-category-delegated** — "Delegated designs (cladding and curtain wall, roofing attachment, joists and trusses, nonstructural and equipment anchorage) state the risk category and importance factors they used, and these match the structural general notes; schools are often assigned above the default, and designers who assume the default under-design."
   What is wrong: wrong trade for this section. Cladding, curtain wall, roofing, joists and trusses are not reviewed in a 22 40 00 package; a fixture reviewer told to check importance factors against the structural general notes is sent down a path that has nothing to review (fixture carriers are not delegated designs in any normal spec). The education overlay has leaked into every section without a scope filter.

2. **edu.fm.default-risk-category** — "Delegated cladding, joist or anchorage design done to the default risk category on a school assigned a higher one → Components under-designed; recalculation and field reinforcement after installation."
   What is wrong: same overlay leak; cladding and joist design is not a plumbing fixture failure mode.

(The four plenum items — d22.plenum-materials, d22.rh.plenum, d22.fm.plastic-in-plenum, ASTM E84 — were scored (d) rather than (c) because they are Division 22 content that is merely inapplicable to a fixture submittal, not a wrong trade; but together with the two (c) items they are six of 66 items that do not belong in this section at all.)
