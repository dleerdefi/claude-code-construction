## 3. Best (b) items

1. **if.03-cold-storage-floors** — "Confirm who: Underfloor heat under freezers on grade — typical furnish 11 41 00 or 13 21 26 / install the electrical or refrigeration trade ... If missed: Freezer built on grade without underfloor heat, or with heating cable never energized or monitored". The baseline covers floor type and slab depression but never raises frost-heave protection under an on-grade freezer, its power/monitoring, or warm-side vapor retarder placement; this is settled at slab pour and is unrecoverable afterward.
2. **if.11-walk-in-sprinklers** — "Heads inside each box where required (dry type in freezers) and above the box, located before panels are cut ... If missed: Sprinklers omitted inside the box, or standard wet heads used inside a freezer". Absent from the baseline entirely; a reviewer following it would never route box dimensions and penetration details to 21 13 00.
3. **d11.service-clearance** — "Manufacturer service and code clearances around each item (coil and filter access, door swings, access to controls and disconnects, clearance to combustibles for fuel-fired and cooking equipment) fit the space shown on the plans with adjacent equipment in place." The baseline checks dimensions against the plan and delivery path but not operating/service clearance with neighbors in place.
4. **11fs.fm.indirect-piping-orphan** — "Indirect waste piping from equipment outlets to floor sinks claimed by neither the equipment contractor nor the plumber → Unconnected drains at the health inspection". The baseline sends floor-sink data to Division 22 but never asks who pipes from the outlet to the receptor.
5. **food.fm.hand-sink-items-unassigned** — "Soap and towel dispensers, splash guards or tempering at hand sinks fall between the foodservice contractor, the plumber and the owner → Missing at the pre-opening inspection". The baseline checks hand-sink location only.

## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) dominate overwhelmingly: 87 of 92 compiled items (95%) are either already produced by the closed-book baseline or are generic boilerplate, and the baseline is in several areas (booster sizing, refrigerants, backflow, energy code, roofing, warranty, standards breadth) deeper than the layer. The layer's marginal value here is five genuine adds, concentrated in scope-orphan interfaces (freezer underfloor heat, sprinklers inside walk-ins, indirect-waste piping ownership, hand-sink accessories) and service clearances, with zero incorrect items.
