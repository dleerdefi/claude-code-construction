## 3. Best (b) items

1. **d05.galvanizing-fabrication** — "Items to be hot-dip galvanized are detailed for the process: vent and drain holes in every closed section and enclosed space, placed where they are acceptable on exposed work; thin, long or asymmetric pieces reviewed for distortion; assemblies sized for the galvanizer's kettle or split with bolted field splices." Why: on a galvanized garage/exterior stair, HSS stringers and pipe rails without vent holes are rejected or rupture at the galvanizer, and visible vent holes on exposed rails are an architectural rejection; the baseline reviewer only checks for pan distortion (#107) and would not look for vent holes or kettle splits on the shop drawings.
2. **05ms.fm.lively-feature-stair** — "Feature stair designed for strength only, with no vibration criterion → A lively stair the owner rejects; stiffening that changes the architecture." Why: the baseline's design-load list (#9) and calc checks (#8, #50) are all strength; a long-span communicating stair in the assumed office project would pass the baseline review with no natural-frequency check, and the fix after installation changes the architecture.
3. **ASTM D6386 / ASTM D7803** — "Standard Practice for Preparation of Zinc (Hot-Dip Galvanized) Coated Iron and Steel Product and Hardware Surfaces for Painting; and for Powder Coating." Why: the baseline knows the paint-on-zinc failure (#79) but lists only SSPC-SP/SSPC-Paint; when a duplex-coating submittal claims a prep method, these are the documents to verify it against instead of answering from memory.

## 4. Every (c) item

- **if.03-steel-anchorage** — "→ Cast-in-place concrete, cast-in anchors and grouting (03 30 00, 03 15 00, 03 60 00) · Gate: foundation_pour — The steel anchor rod layout is approved before footing and pier forms close; the as-built survey is accepted before steel_erection … Confirm who: Grout under column base plates and bearing plates … If missed: Anchor rods, embeds or sleeves not in the forms … Base plates left ungrouted on leveling nuts while the frame is loaded; Anchor rods cast out of position, rotated or at the wrong projection, found as the column is set." What is wrong: this is the 05 12 00 column-anchor-rod interface inherited unchanged. A stair package has no anchor rods, column base plates or grout, and its gate is not the foundation pour; stair embeds are set per level in slab edges and enclosure walls ahead of each pour or grout lift (baseline #36, and compiled's own 05mf.rc.embeds). Marking it critical with a foundation_pour gate would send the stair reviewer to the wrong pour and the wrong document.

## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items COMPILED lacks entirely)

1. #67 / #85 — ASCE 7 §13.5.10 egress-stair drift accommodation (slip or ductile top connection); a known post-earthquake failure, absent from checks, hooks and failure modes.
2. #40 / #80 — handrail extensions vs. door swing and closer arm at landings (08 11 13 / 08 71 00 routing); no door-hardware interface in COMPILED.
3. #19 / #43 / #82 — headroom under landings lost to ducts, sprinkler mains and conduit added after stair approval; no MEP clash routing (23/21/26) at all.
4. #34 — guard post base and handrail bracket anchorage need their own calc pages at the 200 lb load; the most commonly missing calc, not called out.
5. #42 — firestopping (07 84 00) of stringer/landing-support penetrations through rated shaft walls.
6. #60 / #87 — stair hand (mirror-image) vs. A plans.
7. #28 / #56 / #57 — bolt grade, weld symbol and ASTM material grade vs. the Fy in the calc and the S general notes; AWS D1.1, ASTM F3125, A36/A572/A500/A53/A786 missing from the standards list (COMPILED lists only D1.3 and galvanizing/anchor standards).
8. #30 / #68 — noncombustible construction in rated exit enclosures (wood cap, laminate tread) for Type I/II buildings.
9. #101 — K-12: non-climbable guards (no horizontal intermediate rails), abrasive cast nosings, DSA contrast stripes; ironic given the education.k12 overlay delivered storm-shelter items instead.
10. #44 / #47 — stair lighting, exit signs and signage mounting on stair steel needing pre-drilled holes or clips before fabrication.

## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) dominate decisively: 73 of 77 items (95%) either restate what the closed-book baseline already produced or are boilerplate, duplicates and off-scope overlay bleed (storm shelter, OSHA Subpart R, column anchor rods, MEP fireproofing hangers). The three genuine adds (galvanizing vent/drain detailing, feature-stair vibration, D6386/D7803) are real but narrow, while the baseline carries at least ten stair-specific items the layer lacks, including seismic drift accommodation and the door-swing/headroom coordination that fail most often in the field.
