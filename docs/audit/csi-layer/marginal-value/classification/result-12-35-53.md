## 3. Best (b) items

1. **edu.agency-approvals** — "Where a state school-construction agency has jurisdiction, deferred submittals, substitutions and field changes that touch structure, fire and life safety or accessibility are approved by that agency before fabrication, not only by the architect, and the agency's inspector and testing requirements are in the inspection program."
   Why it changes a review: the baseline treats DSA only as a source of anchorage documentation (62) and lab-safety rules (116). On a DSA/TEA-type project a substituted flammable cabinet, lock, or anchorage detail accepted by the architect alone is not approved; the reviewer following the baseline would stamp it and the agency would reopen it at certification. (The matching hook edu.rh.school-agency-review and failure mode edu.fm.agency-change-unapproved carry the same point and were counted as duplicates.)

## 4. Every (c) item

1. **if.10-counter-dispensers** — "→ Toilet accessories (10 28 00) · Gate: procurement_release — Before stone, quartz or solid-surface tops are fabricated / Send them: Shop-cut holes at those positions, and access below the top inside the vanity / Need from them: Counter-mounted dispenser models, hole sizes and positions, and the reservoir and refill clearance they need below the top / Confirm who: Holes for counter-mounted soap dispensers — typical install 12 36 00"
   What is wrong: this is a 12 30 00 vanity/restroom interface inherited unfiltered into 12 35 53. It names "the vanity", stone/quartz tops, and 12 36 00 (Countertops) as the installing section; none applies to epoxy lab tops in a K-12 science room, and it routes the lab casework reviewer to the toilet accessory sub at medium priority. Misleading routing for this section.

## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items COMPILED lacks entirely)

1. **Baseline 36 / 51 / 76** — underslab plumbing coordination: dimensioned rough-in drawing vs plumber's underslab coordination drawing, settled before slab pour (03 30 00). COMPILED's plumbing routing is gated at in_wall_rough_in only; the single most time-critical coordination for this section is absent.
2. **Baseline 23 / 67 / 81 / 109** — vacuum breaker (ASSE 1035/1001) on every hose-end lab faucet and the local cross-connection rule; not mentioned anywhere in COMPILED.
3. **Baseline 7 / 82** — chemical-waste trap material (polypropylene mechanical joint) matching 22 66 53 acid-waste piping; COMPILED never distinguishes chemical waste from sanitary.
4. **Baseline 30 / 42 / 73 / 90** — tall-unit heights vs RCP and 18" sprinkler deflector clearance, routed to 21 13 13; no sprinkler or RCP interface in COMPILED.
5. **Baseline 45 / 58 / 87** — lock cylinder / keying coordination with 08 71 00 and the building master system; COMPILED has only "keying matches the owner's requirement."
6. **Baseline 38 / 92** — fuel-gas piping routing in the chase and master shutoff valve model/location with 23 11 23; COMPILED routes lab gas to 22 63 00 but has no natural-gas interface.
7. **Baseline 70** — GFCI at lab counter receptacles within 6 ft of sinks (NEC 210.8(B)); absent.
8. **Baseline 12 / 59 / 106 / 107** — listed (UL 1275 / FM 6050) flammable cabinets, NFPA 30 and NFPA 45 Ch. 12, and the hazardous storage schedule vs the G-series code analysis / control-area MAQ; COMPILED's hazardous-storage hook is generic and its standards list omits every fire-code and plumbing standard.
9. **Baseline 89** — removable access panels in island/peninsula pipe chases; absent.
10. **Baseline 35 / 114 and 74** — safety glazing in glazed doors (16 CFR 1201) and aisle width / egress between fixed casework (NFPA 45); both absent.

## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) = 95 of 97 items dominate overwhelmingly; the only net-new item is the state-school-agency approval routing, and the one error is a vanity dispenser interface inherited from 12 30 00. For this section the knowledge layer is at best a reformatting of what the baseline already produced, and it is thinner than the baseline on the lab-specific items (underslab coordinates, backflow, chemical waste traps, sprinkler clearance, keying, NFPA/UL standards).
