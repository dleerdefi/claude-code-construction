## 3. Best (b) items

1. **if.11-stage-fire-curtain** — "Fire curtain or water curtain release devices and stage smoke vent operators, with their release and reset requirements … If missed: Fire curtain or stage vents installed with no release signal from detection; fails the fire authority's acceptance test." K-12 auditoriums with a proscenium stage carry IBC 410 curtain/vent release; the baseline treats the stage only as an acoustic and high-ceiling detection problem, so a reviewer would not ask 11 61 00 for release signals.
2. **if.27-errcs-fire-alarm-monitoring** — "Need from them: Supervisory outputs for amplifier, antenna, power and battery trouble at a point the fire alarm can reach … If missed: Signal boosters installed with no fire alarm monitoring points." ERRCS is absent from the baseline entirely; where IFC 510 applies, the BDA supervisory points must land on the FACP and nobody carries the modules unless asked.
3. **if.21-release-control** — "Need from them: Release valve or actuator models, the releasing panels they are listed with, and the release logic (single or double interlock, cross-zoned detection, time delay and abort) … If missed: The releasing panel and the agent valve actuators are not listed together." Baseline #26 only puts a monitor module on pre-action valves; the listing-together and interlock-logic question is the one that actually fails a preaction/clean-agent room at acceptance.
4. **if.08-fire-alarm-doors** — "Need from them: The doors that release, unlock, close or stop on alarm (hold-opens, fail-safe locks, delayed egress, stair re-entry, rolling fire doors and shutters, operators) … If missed: Rated coiling door's release never tied to the fire alarm, or its operator holds the door open on alarm." Baseline #29/#42 route only to the 08 71 00 hardware schedule; coiling fire shutters live in 08 33 00 and are a routine K-12 miss at cafeteria serving lines.

## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items COMPILED lacks)

1. #8 / #57 — SLC loop calculations: addresses per loop vs panel capacity, loop resistance/capacitance vs manufacturer limits, Class A/X isolator placement, reconciled to the riser.
2. #24 / #65 / #108 — Pull stations: count and 200 ft travel vs the IBC 907.2.3 single-station exception, AHJ acceptance of the exception, tamper covers with local sounders against pranks. Compiled never mentions manual stations.
3. #43 / #72 / #90 — Lockdown / mass notification priority vs fire alarm voice, NFPA 72 Ch. 24 risk analysis, and which system wins. The most K-12-specific item in the baseline and wholly absent from an education-overlaid compile.
4. #23 / #84 — Device selection by room: heat detectors in kitchens and dirty mechanical rooms, beam detectors in high gyms, no smoke detectors near cooking or shop dust; nuisance alarms leading to disabled zones.
5. #31 / #85 — Spare capacity: NAC/booster loading against power supply rating with the specified margin, SLC address spare, headroom for devices the AHJ adds at walkthrough.
6. #30 / #76 — Wiring method: FPL/FPLR/FPLP ratings in return-air ceilings, NEC 760 raceway sharing, red boxes and covers.
7. #14 / #33 — Mounting heights against ADA 2010 / ICC A117.1 (pull station 42-48 in., strobe lens 80-96 in.), ceiling detector spacing on the actual ceiling construction.
8. #32 / #66 — NFPA 72 Ch. 26 communicator path count (two paths vs single path with supervision interval) and whether the AHJ accepts single IP/cellular; compiled's monitoring-path check is only "arranged so monitoring is live."
9. #96 / #18 — Turnover package: programming passwords, software, as-builts, owner training; compiled test reports stop at the record of completion.
10. #98, #101-#107 — Standards list: compiled gives three standards; baseline adds NEC 760, UL 268/268A (7th edition cooking-nuisance test), UL 521, UL 38, UL 464, UL 1480, UL 1971, UL 1481, UL 1424, UL 827, UL 2572, UL 2075, ASME A17.1, UL 1479/ASTM E814. ## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) dominate at 95%: the baseline already produced every review check, reconciliation, regulatory hook, submittal-content bullet and standard in the compile, and 16 items are Division 01 or Division 28 generic noise that an FA reviewer would not act on. The four (b) items are all coordination routings to trades outside the FA contractor's usual lens (stage rigging, coiling fire doors, ERRCS, releasing panels), and against them the compile is missing SLC calcs, pull stations, lockdown/mass-notification priority, device selection by room, spare capacity, wiring method, mounting heights and most of the standards list.
