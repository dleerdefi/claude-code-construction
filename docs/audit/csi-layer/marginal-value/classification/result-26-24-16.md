## 3. Best (b) items

1. "**26ds.grounding-neutral** — The neutral is bonded to ground only at the service and at each separately derived system; downstream panelboards have isolated neutrals; transformer secondaries have their system bonding jumper and grounding electrode conductor shown." The baseline reviews neutral rating and ground bars but never asks whether each panel is ordered as a downstream (isolated-neutral) panel versus service-entrance with bonding strap; a bonded neutral downstream is an objectionable-current and GFP-misoperation defect that reads as a harmless checkbox on the cut sheet.
2. "**26ds.rh.ground-fault** — Where does the adopted electrical code require ground-fault protection of equipment (by service or feeder disconnect rating and voltage, and additional levels in health care), and where does it prohibit it?" A 1000 A+ 480Y/277 distribution panelboard main needs feeder GFP unless the upstream device provides it; the baseline lists UL 1053 as a standard but never asks the question, so an electronic-trip-with-GFP main (bigger frame, deeper can) is missed.
3. "**26ds.rh.arc-energy-reduction** — Does the adopted electrical code require an arc energy reduction method on circuit breakers or fuses above a rating threshold, which methods qualify ..., and must the method be performance-tested at installation?" 1200 A panelboard mains fall under this; the baseline has no 240.87 question anywhere.
4. "**26pb.rc.circuits** — Every circuit on the power and lighting plans appears on its panel schedule under the same panel and number, and no scheduled circuit is missing from the plans." The baseline only checks submittal against drawing schedule; a schedule that disagrees with the plans produces a panel short of breakers that passes the baseline review.
5. "**if.26-errcs-power** → In-building radio and antenna systems (27 53 19) ... Send them: Dedicated circuit on the source the radio coverage code requires, with the circuit protection and identification that code calls for." No ERRCS routing in the baseline; the dedicated, identified circuit on the required source must land on a specific panel schedule before release.
6. "**if.26-telecom-room-power** → Structured cabling and equipment room fittings (27 10 00, 27 11 00) ... Need from them: Rack layouts, equipment loads and plug types, UPS sizing and who furnishes it." No telecom routing in the baseline; rack plug types (L6-30 vs. 5-20) decide pole count and trip in the IDF panel and arrive after the panel is released.

## 5. Reverse gaps (baseline items COMPILED lacks)

1. Breaker interrupting ratings differ by system voltage; the table must show the kAIC at the panel's voltage (baseline 7).
2. Bus material (copper vs. aluminum, plating) and 200% neutral where scheduled, confirmed in the order code, not the option list (baseline 5, 18, 19, 59).
3. Nameplate / "fed from" text matched character-for-character to the one-line; shop names on engraved nameplates (baseline 9, 42, 62).
4. Single-source manufacturer across switchboard, panelboards and breakers where series ratings or coordination are relied on; VE of the switchboard after panel approval (baseline 24, 44, 57).
5. Code-driven breaker types by location: GFCI 210.8(B), GFEP heat trace, HACR, SWD/HID, shunt-trip for hood suppression and elevator (baseline 22, 50).
6. Main overcurrent device requirement vs. 408.36 exceptions and the spec's "main breaker on every panel" clause (baseline 25, 47).
7. Series-rating field marking (110.22(B)/(C), 240.86) and the exact tested upstream device catalog number (baseline 46).
8. Top vs. bottom feed matched to feeder stub direction; 312.6 bending space (baseline 63).
9. Energy-code branch-circuit metering (IECC C405.12 / 90.1 8.4.3) that puts factory metering in the panels (baseline 55, 10).
10. Product standards beyond UL 67: UL 489, UL 50/50E, NEMA PB 1/PB 1.1, NEMA AB 1, NEMA 250, UL 1449, UL 943/1053 (baseline 68-74); and district-standard manufacturer / keyed-alike / locked-room / student-inaccessible location checks (baseline 36, 49, 76).

## 6. Verdict

(a)+(d) dominate at 94 of 100; the baseline already covers nearly everything panelboard-specific, and roughly a third of the compiled page is switchboard, service-entrance, floor-standing or healthcare material inherited because no 26 24 16 profile exists. The six (b) items are real but come from the division-level interfaces and hooks (ERRCS, telecom, feeder GFP, 240.87, isolated neutral, plans-vs-schedule), while the baseline's product-level depth (bus material, voltage-specific kAIC, nameplates, single-source, breaker types by location, the UL/NEMA standard set) is entirely absent from the layer.
